# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Remember there is a person on the other end.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Baa: contributing, reporting a bug, asking a question:
to be free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Review the code, not the
  person who wrote it.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from
  it.
- Remember that "I don't understand this" is usually a documentation bug, not a
  failing of the person saying it.
- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith until you have
  real evidence otherwise.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer listed on the repository
profile. Reports are handled confidentially and reviewed promptly.

Maintainers will follow these guidelines when deciding what to do:

1. **Correction.** A private, written note explaining what was wrong and why.
   A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A warning, with consequences for continued behaviour. No
   interaction with the people involved for a stated period.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban from public interaction with the project.

Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may face consequences
determined by other maintainers.
